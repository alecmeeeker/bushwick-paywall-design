Becoming a Bushwick Daily member: the whole flow

Design v2.1 mockups, 2026-10-13: phone (iPhone 13) first, then desktop (1440). Under each step: the words on screen, extracted from the mockup; what the reader can do and where it leads; how to leave. Nothing here is live; card forms are Stripe test mode. Spec: PROPOSAL.md V2 and V2.1; text twin: FLOW.md.

Gate → Email → Code | Plans → Payment → Success → Story

A member gets the code; everyone else gets the plans. The sheet opens only after the reader acts, always on Monthly.
